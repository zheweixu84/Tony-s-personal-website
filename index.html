01 / INTRODUCTION

About Me.

Hi, I'm Tony Xu, a Design student at the University of Auckland, New Zealand.

My interests lie in product design, physical prototyping, and interactive experiences. I enjoy exploring how creative ideas can be transformed into tangible products and experiences that people can see, touch, and interact with.

Through experimentation, 3D modelling, and hands-on making, I aim to bridge the gap between ideas and real-world experiences. Whether creating something entirely new or improving an existing design, my goal is to make technology and physical objects more engaging, accessible, and meaningful to people.

01 / MY STORY

Shanghai to Auckland.

I'm originally from Shanghai, China, and I've spent the past seven years living in New Zealand. Experiencing life across different places and cultures has shaped the way I notice everyday objects, communicate with people, and think about belonging.

I'm studying for a Bachelor of Design at the University of Auckland. Through different projects, I've become especially interested in making things that exist beyond a screen — products, prototypes, and interactive experiences people can actually use.

02 / HOW I WORK

Ideas deserve to be made.

I enjoy the full process, from an early sketch to a 3D model and then a physical prototype. Testing a material, adjusting a mechanism, or seeing someone interact with an object often teaches me more than a polished concept alone.

I'm interested in both creating something entirely new and rethinking something that already exists. For me, the goal is always to make an idea useful, engaging, and meaningful in the real world.

03 / BEYOND THE STUDIO

Curiosity beyond the studio.

Photography and painting help me notice details, explore colour, and tell stories from a different perspective. I also enjoy working with people through student association activities, marketing, and event coordination — experiences that bring a practical, collaborative side to my design practice.

Outside of design projects, I enjoy photography, exploring new places, personal style, and the occasional bubble tea. I am also very much a dog person.

04 / COMMUNICATION & PERSPECTIVE

Between languages and ideas.

I speak Mandarin and English, with basic German. Living between Shanghai and Auckland has made cross-cultural communication a meaningful part of how I collaborate and approach different audiences.
